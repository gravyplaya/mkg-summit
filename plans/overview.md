**🌟 Overview**

**The Innovators Summit** is a **half-day regional event** celebrating creativity, entrepreneurship, and innovation across the **Muskegon Lakeshore and surrounding areas**. The summit will bring together 300+ entrepreneurs, creators, business leaders, and community partners to **be informed, inspired, and encouraged to be bold and innovative**. This will be the who’s who among leaders, innovators, movers, and shakers. Big and small all in one place\! 

We chose the name **The Innovators Summit** intentionally.

*Summit* reflects our goal of bringing people together from across the region—summoning leaders, creators, and change-makers to a shared place and moment for a focused, half-day experience.

*Innovators* is equally deliberate. This event isn’t about a single innovation or a narrow definition of technology. It’s about people—the people who have already innovated, those who are eager to innovate, the dreamers and the doers.

The Innovators Summit is about connection. It’s about bringing bold thinkers together—those who dare to question the status quo, suggest something different, and imagine what’s next.

**🎤 Event Highlights**

* **Keynote Speaker:** National-level innovator sharing insight on creativity, resilience, and the future of innovation  
* **Panel Discussion:** Regional leaders discussing innovation in business, community, and technology  
* **Breakout Sessions:** Hands-on sessions exploring funding, design thinking, growth strategy, and collaboration  
* **Exhibitor Showcase:** Local innovators, startups, and resource organizations. This will include the area’s large manufacturing innovators as well as the Innovator’s Alley with the smaller businesses showcasing their innovations.   
* **Interactive Experiences:** Demos, creativity zones, and networking activities
